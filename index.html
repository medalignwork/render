What MedAlign is

A platform that matches patients with clinics

A patient completes a short health assessment. MedAlign's matching engine considers the patient's goals, symptoms, treatment interests, medical considerations, preferred location, lifestyle and desired therapies. It then shows personalized clinic matches ranked by fit, with a match percentage.

Trust and transparency

How MedAlign works

These are the platform's stated practices and the limits patients should understand.

✓

Ranked by fit, not ad spend

Matches are ranked by how closely a clinic's services line up with the patient's answers. A clinic cannot pay to move up the list.

＄

Free for patients

The assessment and clinic matches cost patients nothing, and no credit card is required. Clinics are billed per qualified consultation, with no long-term contracts.

↗

Clinic connection by request

A clinic receives a scheduled consultation only after a patient explicitly asks to connect. Patient details are not broadcast to every clinic.

How the platform earns money: Clinics are billed only when a patient who fits their chosen criteria explicitly asks to connect and a consultation is scheduled. The platform earns money when a real match happens.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MedAlign legit?

MedAlign (Med Align) is a patient-to-clinic matching platform at getmedalign.com. It is free for patients, states that matches are ranked by fit and not by ad spend, and says clearly that it is not a healthcare provider. Patients should review the details and check a clinic's credentials before booking.

Is MedAlign really free?

Yes, for patients. The assessment and clinic matches are free, and no credit card is required. Clinics pay per qualified consultation.

Is MedAlign a healthcare provider, and is its advice medical advice?

No. MedAlign is a matching platform. Matches are informational and are not medical advice, diagnosis or a clinical referral.

What happens to my information when I take the assessment?

No account is needed. MedAlign asks for location and contact details at the very end, after you have seen your matches, and states that it sends no spam and is HIPAA compliant. A clinic receives a scheduled consultation only after you explicitly ask to connect with it.

Is MedAlign the same as medalign.com or AlignMed?

No. MedAlign (Med Align) at getmedalign.com is the patient-to-clinic matching platform. It is not the medalign.com company that works on physician payment models, the Stanford MedAlign benchmark dataset for electronic health record research, or the posture apparel brand AlignMed.

Important: MedAlign is not a healthcare provider. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Its matches are informational and are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a clinical referral. Treatment decisions are made between the patient and the clinic they choose. MedAlign does not guarantee patients to clinics.
A quick checklist

Questions to ask about any matching platform

These questions apply to MedAlign and to any similar service.

Is the business model clear?

Is it clear how the platform makes money?

Are its limits clear?

Does it say plainly that it is not a healthcare provider, and avoid guarantees about outcomes?

Are patients in control?

Are rankings based on fit instead of payment? Are personal details requested only when needed? Can patients contact the clinic directly before committing?
